Service questions

How much does custom paint or body work cost?

Every paint and body job is quoted individually because size, damage and finish vary. Text photos and your vehicle details to (804) 647-1222 for pricing.

Can you paint just a mirror, emblem or bumper?

Yes. Paint-matched mirrors, custom color-matched emblems and single parts are regular work at the shop.

What is candy paint?

Candy is a tinted, see-through color coat sprayed over a base such as metallic, flake or even carbon fiber, which gives the finish its deep, wet look.

Who installs the starlight headliners?

Starlight headliners are in-house custom work by ATB Kustoms, the airbrush, lettering and interior side of the shop.
