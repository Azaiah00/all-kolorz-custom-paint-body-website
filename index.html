All Kolorz has been painting cars on Richmond’s Southside since 2009. Walk the lot on Belt Boulevard and you will see it all at once: a fender getting its jambs sprayed, a Mustang waiting on paint-matched mirrors, a set of motorcycle side covers glowing candy orange in the sun.

Some of it is collision work that should never be noticed again. Some of it is meant to stop traffic at a car show. Both get the same attention, and custom work is one of one.

Straight answers

Still wondering? Text the shop at (804) 647-1222.

Where is All Kolorz located?

All Kolorz Custom Paint & Body is at 232 E Belt Blvd, Richmond, VA 23224, on Belt Boulevard in Southside Richmond.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. The shop is closed Saturday and Sunday; evening car shows are announced on Instagram.

How do I get a quote?

Text clear photos plus your vehicle year, make and model to (804) 647-1222, call, send a DM to @all_kolorz_ on Instagram, or fill out the quote form on this site. Every job is priced individually.

Can you match my factory paint color?

Yes. Color matching is what the shop is known for, from paint-matched mirrors and emblems to panels blended back into the rest of the car.

Do you work on trucks, SUVs and motorcycles?

Yes. The shop handles cars, trucks and SUVs, from daily drivers to luxury SUVs and classics, and paints motorcycle fairings, tanks and side covers.

How much does a starlight headliner cost?

ATB Kustoms starlight packages start at $250 for 250 stars and run to $900 for 1,200 stars, with larger counts priced per vehicle. Prices vary by vehicle type.

Do you host car shows?

Yes. All Kolorz co-hosts car shows with Dreamchasers Auto Club RVA at the shop. The next one is Dreams vs Nightmares: Halloween Edition on Saturday, October 24, 2026, 6 to 10 PM.
